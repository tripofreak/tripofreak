Our Story

Ten years.
Three restarts.
One obsession.

Tripofreak didn't start with AI. It started with a frustration — that every travel tool tells you what to do, never why. We've been trying to fix that since 2014.

The journey

2014
The idea is born
Tripofreak was conceived — a travel platform that thinks like a local, not a guidebook. The first version was built and quietly launched. No users, no traction. Shut down.
2016
@tripofreak goes live on Instagram
A second attempt — this time building the brand through content. The Instagram account was born. It worked for a while, then quietly faded again.
2024–25
AI changes everything
Large language models finally made it possible to build what we always imagined — a planner that doesn't just list places, but explains every decision. The third attempt began.
2026
Tripofreak relaunches — for real this time
Built from scratch. AI-powered. Free to use. Every itinerary explained, every risk flagged, every hidden gem surfaced. Persistency was always the key.

What we believe

Most travel tools give you a list. Tripofreak gives you a reason. Every activity is grouped by proximity so you don't waste hours in transit. Every day is sequenced so crowds work in your favour. Every risk — scams, tourist traps, seasonal closures — is flagged before you leave home.

We call the unexpected finds Freaky Finds. The places most travellers miss because they never appear in any top-10 list. That's the heart of what Tripofreak has always been about.

We're not a booking platform. We're not paid to recommend hotels. We're just obsessively building the travel planner we always wished existed.
